# Update Log

## 2026-11-19

* **Creation**: Added the **burn–coast–burn cruise**, step 3 of [Burn–coast–burn trips](/simulation/burn-coast-burn.md). `TripPlanner.CruiseStyle.BurnCoastBurn` drives the search and builds the five-arc profile: a quintic break out of the departure sphere of influence → one prograde finite burn about the Sun at `BurnLevelG` → the heliocentric Lambert coast → one retrograde finite brake about the Sun → the existing conic or hybrid capture. The chain is solved **backwards** from the capture chain's entry state, so every seam is continuous in position and velocity and the arrival is exact: the brake is integrated in reverse from the entry, the coast in reverse from the brake's start, and the departure burn in reverse from the coast's start, while the quintic matches the burn's start state (a quintic can match any state). The brake spends the handover impulse the coast skeleton leaves at the target's sphere of influence, so the capture chain is handed its entry state with **no impulse at the target SOI**. A burn along the instantaneous velocity can only change the speed, and the handover is not a pure speed change, so the brake's thrust direction is held fixed along the change the chain needs and the direction and duration are iterated until the burn begins exactly where the coast ends; the direction opposes the arrival velocity, and a trip time whose coast arrives slower than the target's frame (which would need an acceleration) is rejected with its reason. The departure burn is sized from the coast's departure speed above the local escape speed, not from a share of the trip time: its delta-v is level-independent and its duration scales as `1/level`. It begins outside the departure sphere of influence and never crosses it; a level or trip time that would force a crossing is rejected. `FiniteBurnArc` gained an optional fixed thrust direction and `SimulateBackward`, the reverse-flow integrator the planner needs. At `0 g` the style delegates to `CruiseStyle.Coast` exactly. Measured at the epoch from Gateway to Mars at 90 days and 0.3 g: a 4.73-day break, a 78-minute departure burn of 13.7 km/s, an 84-day coast, a 3.1-hour brake of 19.2 km/s, and the 3.5 km/s conic capture, 50 km/s in total; seams continuous to under a centimetre and 10⁻⁵ m/s; the flight arrives 0.1 m and 0.0001 m/s from the reference state and holds the parking orbit. * **Creation**: Added the zero-revolution Lambert solver to Core. `LambertSolver.SolveShortWay(r₁, r₂, Δt, μ)` returns `v₁`, `v₂`, and the swept angle for the prograde short-way arc — less than 180° counter-clockwise about the ecliptic pole — reusing `KeplerPropagator.Stumpff` for the universal variables and solving `z = αχ²` by bracket and bisection.
