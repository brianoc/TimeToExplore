* **Update**: `F` now **frames** the selected body instead of only pointing at it. The camera still does not move the ship, but the **render origin** moves to the body: while focused, every body position is computed relative to the selected body, which sits at `(0,0,0)` at its true size, and the camera orbits it at four radii. This bypasses the float-precision limit that kept an interplanetary planet a marker even when the camera aimed at it: at 1 AU a true-radius body is smaller than one float step at the ship origin. * **Update**: Added the **departure climb split search** behind an internal `TripPlanner.UseDepartureSplit` flag, **off by default**. When it is on, the planner chooses the escape conic's hyperbolic excess `v∞` that minimises the plan's total delta-v at the requested duration instead of taking the parabolic climb whenever it fits. The measured curve is not unimodal, so the search is a **grid** — `0, 1, 2, 3, 6, 10, 16` km/s, capped at 16 — then **two bisection refinements** between the best point and its neighbours; a candidate whose climb cannot fit the time is skipped.
